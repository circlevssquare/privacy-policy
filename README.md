CIRCLE VS SQUARE - PRIVACY POLICY

App: Circle vs Square (Android package: com.circlevssquare.game)
Developer: Goyat Developer Studios
Last updated: 21 September 2026
Contact: ajju52028@gmail.com

This policy explains what information Circle vs Square ("the App", "we", "us") uses, where it is stored, and who else may receive it. There are no accounts in the App, and it does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.


1. STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The App saves the following in its local storage on your device. It stays there unless you use one of the features described in sections 2 to 4:

- The player name you optionally type in.
- Your results: games played, won and lost per difficulty, points, level and achievement titles, your fastest win time per difficulty, and your total play time.
- Daily reward progress (the date of your last claim and your streak day).
- Your settings: sound, vibration, and whether you have already seen the tutorial.
- A randomly generated device ID that contains no personal information. It is created by the App itself (it is not your Android Advertising ID or any hardware identifier) and is used only to tell the two devices apart during an online match.

Playing against the Bot or in Local 2 Players mode sends none of this to us. (Ads, when shown, are covered in section 3.)


2. ONLINE "PLAY WITH FRIENDS"

When you create or join a room, the App connects to Firebase Realtime Database, a service operated by Google. To relay the match between you and your friend, the room stores: the room code, both player names, the board position, whose turn it is, the latest move, the result, the options chosen for the match (Pro board, time limit), your random device ID and a creation time. This is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. As with any internet service, Google's servers also receive technical data such as your IP address when your device connects, and Google's servers may be located outside your country.

This data is used only to run the match. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. To connect, the App downloads Firebase's code from Google (gstatic.com) the first time you use online mode.

Anyone who knows a room code can reach that room's data, so share your code only with the friend you want to play with, and do not put personal information in your player name.


3. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner ads may appear at the top and bottom of some screens). To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device and app information, approximate location and how you interact with ads, and may show personalized ads where permitted. This is handled by the ad network under its own policies and is separate from the online-match data in section 2.

In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the UK), the App shows Google's consent form before loading ads, and ads are only requested as your choice allows. You can also reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device's system settings (on Android, usually under Settings > Privacy or Google > Ads).

More information: "How Google uses information from apps that use its services" - https://policies.google.com/technology/partner-sites


4. SHARING, NOTIFICATIONS AND LINKS

- Share: when you share a result or achievement, the image is created on your device and handed to your device's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us. The library used to create the image (html2canvas) is normally included with the App; only if that copy is unavailable may the App download it from a public CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), which can then see your IP address. Once you send the image or text through another app, that app's own privacy policy applies. Some share options open WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Facebook, SMS or email with your message filled in, only when you tap them.
- Notifications: on devices that support it, the App may ask permission during an online match to notify you when it is your turn while the App is in the background. These notifications are created on your device; no personal data is sent to us for them. You can switch them off in your device settings.
- Social links: the YouTube, Instagram and Facebook buttons open those services, and their policies apply there.


5. PERMISSIONS AND DEVICE FEATURES

The App uses: internet access (online play and ads), network state (to check that a connection is available), vibration (haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings), and the advertising ID (used by AdMob for advertising, see section 3). The App keeps the screen awake while a match is in progress. None of these are used to collect personal information for us. The App does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.


6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is a general-audience abstract strategy game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age required by the law of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it.


7. SECURITY

Data sent to online-match servers travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Rooms are identified by random 5-character codes, and the App does not have a user database or accounts. Data stored on your device stays inside the App's private storage. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we keep the amount of data we handle to a minimum.


8. KEEPING AND DELETING YOUR DATA

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from your device settings, permanently deletes everything listed in section 1.

Online-match data: a room is deleted when the host cancels it or a player leaves the match. If the App is closed unexpectedly or the connection drops, the room may stay on the server until it is removed. To ask us to delete a room's data, email us the 5-character room code (and roughly when you played); because there are no accounts, we cannot find your data without it.

Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted under Google's own policies (see the link in section 3).


9. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Contact us and we will help. Because there are no accounts, the only data we could hold about you is online-room data.


10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision, and the current version is always available in the App under Settings > Privacy Policy.
