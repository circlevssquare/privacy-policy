Privacy Policy
Last updated: 17 September 2026
This policy explains what information Circle vs Square ("the App") stores and why. The App is designed to work fully offline for solo play against the Bot; it only talks to a server when you choose Online "Play with Friends" mode, and it may show ads.
Information stored only on your device
•	The name you optionally enter to identify yourself in-game.
•	Match history: games played, won and lost per difficulty, points, level and unlocked achievement titles.
•	Your best (fastest) time to defeat the Bot per difficulty, and your total lifetime play time.
•	Your sound and vibration on/off preferences, and whether you've already seen the tutorial.
•	A randomly generated device ID that contains no personal information, used only to tell devices apart during an online match.
None of the above ever leaves your device unless you turn on Online mode (see below), use the Share feature, or an ad is shown (see Advertising below).
Online "Play with Friends" mode
When you create or join an online room, your chosen player name and your game moves are sent to our Firebase Realtime Database purely to relay moves between your device and your opponent's device for that match. This data is not used for advertising, not sold, and is periodically cleared from the database.
Sharing a result or achievement
The Share feature uses your device's own native share sheet (or saves an image to your device). Nothing is uploaded to us when you use it.
Advertising
The App shows ads through Google AdMob (and/or its partner ad networks). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data. This data is collected and processed by Google/AdMob under their own privacy policy — see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (e.g. GDPR/UK, or Google's "Families" ad policies), a consent prompt will be shown before personalized ads are enabled, and you can manage ad personalization from your device's system settings.
Children's privacy
The App is a general-audience abstract strategy game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it.
Your choices
Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from your device settings, permanently deletes all information listed above from your device. To request deletion of data associated with an online match still held on our server, or to opt out of ad personalization, contact us using the details below.
Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.
