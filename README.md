Privacy Policy
Last updated: 24 September 2026
App: Circle vs Square (Android package: com.circlevssquare.game)
Developer: Goyat Developer Studios

This policy explains what information Circle vs Square ("the App", "we", "us") uses, where it is stored, and who else may receive it. There are no accounts in the App, and it does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The App has no in-app purchases.

In short: playing against the Bot or in Local 2 Players mode keeps everything on your device. Online "Play with Friends" sends match data to Google's Firebase service so the two devices stay in sync. The App is supported by one small banner ad served by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID and other technical data.

1. Stored only on your device
The App saves the following in its local storage on your device. It stays there unless you use one of the features described in sections 2 to 4:

The player name you optionally type in.
Your results: games played, won and lost per difficulty, points, level and achievement titles, your fastest win time per difficulty, and your total play time.
Daily reward progress (the date of your last claim and your streak day).
Your settings: sound, vibration, and whether you have already seen the tutorial.
A randomly generated device ID that contains no personal information. It is created by the App itself (it is not an advertising ID or any hardware identifier) and is used only to tell the two devices apart during an online match.
The 5-character code of the online room you created most recently, kept only until that room has been cleaned up (see section 8).
Playing against the Bot (Easy, Normal or Hard, on the standard or Pro board) or in Local 2 Players mode — including using Hints and Undo — sends none of this to us. The banner ad described in section 3 is shown in every mode, but it does not receive this data.

2. Online "Play with Friends"
When you create or join a room (standard or Pro board, with or without the optional 15-minute time limit), the App connects to Firebase Realtime Database, a service operated by Google. To relay the match between you and your friend, the room stores: the room code, both player names, each player's random device ID, the board position, whose turn it is, the latest move, the result and how the match ended, the options chosen for the match (Pro board, time limit), whether a move was undone, whether a player has left, a marker for "Play Again" rounds, and a creation time. This is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. As with any internet service, Google's servers also receive technical data such as your IP address when your device connects, and Google's servers may be located outside your country.

This data is used only to run the match. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. Your friend sees the name you enter and you see theirs; the App has no chat or voice feature. To connect, the App downloads Firebase's code from Google (gstatic.com) the first time you use online mode.

Anyone who knows a room code can reach that room's data, so share your code only with the friend you want to play with, and do not put personal information in your player name.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App is free and is supported by advertising. It shows one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, on every screen (main menu, during a match and on the results screen), served by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google. The App does not show full-screen, pop-up, video or rewarded ads, and ads appear only in the installed mobile app, not in a web browser.

To choose, show and measure these ads, AdMob (through the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the App) may collect and use: your device's advertising ID; your IP address and the rough location Google derives from it; device and software information such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size; information about the App such as its name and version; and ad and interaction data such as whether an ad was shown or tapped, plus diagnostic data. Google may store identifiers or other data on your device for these purposes.

The App does not send AdMob your player name, your results or achievements, your random device ID, or any online room details. We do not receive personal information about you from AdMob; we only see summary reports of ad performance and earnings.

Consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland (or another place where consent is required), the App shows Google's consent form when you open it, so you can choose how your data may be used for ads, including whether ads are personalized. The App does not request an ad until this consent step, where it applies, allows it.

Your choices. You can change ad settings at any time in your device's Android/Google settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads): reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization. Ads may still be shown, but they will not be personalized. Uninstalling the App stops all ad data collection by the App.

Advertising ID permission. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK uses Android's advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID). The ads in the App are general-audience ads, and the App is not directed at children (see section 6).

See Google's own policies for how AdMob handles this data: Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy), "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and AdMob & Firebase data usage (support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

4. Sharing, notifications and links
Share: when you share a result, achievement or invitation, the image is created on your device and handed to your device's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us. The card or message can include your player name (if you enter one when sharing), the match result or achievement, and a link to the App's store page. The library used to create the image (html2canvas) is normally included with the App; only if that copy is unavailable may the App download it from a public CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), which can then see your IP address. Once you send the image or text through another app, that app's own privacy policy applies. Some share options open WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Facebook, SMS or email with your message filled in, only when you tap them. The App writes to your clipboard only when you tap Copy, and never reads it.
Notifications: on devices that support it, the App may ask permission during an online match to notify you when it is your turn while the App is in the background. These notifications are created on your device; no personal data is sent to us for them. You can switch them off in your device settings.
Social links: the YouTube, Instagram and Facebook buttons open those services, and their policies apply there.
5. Permissions and device features
The App uses: internet access (online play and ads), vibration (haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings), and the advertising ID and network-status permissions needed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (see section 3). The App keeps the screen awake while a match is in progress. None of these are used to collect personal information for us. The App does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

6. Children's privacy
The App is a general-audience abstract strategy game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age required by the law of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it.

7. Security
Data sent to online-match servers travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Rooms are identified by random 5-character codes, and the App does not have a user database or accounts. Data stored on your device stays inside the App's private storage. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we keep the amount of data we handle to a minimum.

8. Keeping and deleting your data
Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from your device settings, permanently deletes everything listed in section 1.

Online-match data: a room is deleted when the host cancels it or a player leaves the match. If the host's connection drops before anyone has joined, the room is removed automatically. If the App is closed unexpectedly during a match, the host's device deletes the room the next time the App is opened with an internet connection; until then, or if the App is never opened again, the room may remain on the server. Google may keep technical connection logs for a limited time under its own policies. To ask us to delete a room's data, email us the 5-character room code (and roughly when you played); because there are no accounts, we cannot find your data without it.

Ad data: data collected by AdMob is held by Google under its own retention rules, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 3.

9. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Contact us and we will help. Because there are no accounts, the only data we could hold about you is online-room data. For ads, your main controls are the consent form (where it is shown) and your device's ad settings described in section 3; because AdMob collects that data directly, you can also make requests about it to Google.

10. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new ad formats or features that use data in new ways. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision, and the current version is always available in the App under Settings → Privacy Policy.
